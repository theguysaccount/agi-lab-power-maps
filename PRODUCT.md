# Product

**User.** Readers, journalists, policy staff and lab employees asking "who actually controls OpenAI / Anthropic / …?".

**Painful job.** The answer is scattered across charters, filings and news; the essays answer it beautifully but on two long
pages, without per-claim dates.

**Promise.** One page per lab: who holds ultimate control, through what lever, how concentrated it is (an editorial score,
labelled as such), every fact with its source, and when it was last verified.

**Wedge.** Search and AI-answer citations for "who controls OpenAI", "who owns Anthropic", "xAI ownership", with a share
card per lab. The essays stay the destination for the argument; these pages are the reference.

**Activation metric.** A visitor opens a cited source or follows through to an essay (GoatCounter once enabled).

**First revenue event.** None. Slot: `SUPPORT_URL` (donation or grant link).

**Won't do.** Add claims, update facts on its own, solicit confidential information or encourage breaching agreements,
or host the essays' recruiting call as its own message.
